- **Rules and access.** The DNR issues debris-burning permits in the north. Local fire departments or governments issue them in the south. The state fire marshal and local fire chiefs can impose emergency restrictions. The governor can restrict fire use on or near forest lands by proclamation. No current statewide proclamation surfaced. The forest-land permit requirement depends on snow cover, and local ordinances and EGLE air quality rules also apply. Direct curl to the DNR permit guide, April lookup release, fire alert, EGLE brochure and November safety article hit Akamai 403. Indexed official copies confirm the lookup workflow, restrictions in some areas without county decisions, the snow-cover rule and material limits.
