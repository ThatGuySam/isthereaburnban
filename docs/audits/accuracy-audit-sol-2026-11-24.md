# Burn-ban data and claims audit — November 24, 2026

## 1. Takeaway

Bexar County announced a lift on November 3. Both Texas state feeds and the beta still say the ban is active. The beta also misses published restrictions in Connecticut and Brunswick and Nottoway counties, Virginia.  
Iberia and Red River parishes return `unknown` in the API, but the map’s code gives them green. Miami and four Indiana counties illustrate how a green headline can conceal standing restrictions.  
Preserve issuer, jurisdiction, activity, legal dates and source freshness separately. Require direct-authority evidence before presenting an all-clear.  
I compared 56 counties across 19 states, covering all six full feeds and Iowa, the seventh state labeled live. Preserve Iowa’s positive-only policy.  
Beta REST worked. Beta MCP returned `Invalid Host` and the documented apex endpoints returned 404. The checkout contains the MCP hostname fix.
