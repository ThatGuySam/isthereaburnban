Wood Stove Curtailment Status

CURTAILMENT STATUS: 
Wood burning curtailment for the 26/27 season resumes 9/1/2026. 11/24 is NOT a wood burning curtailment day. 11/25, 11/26, 11/27 are curtailment days . Using uncertified wood stoves in the Nonattainment Area is prohibited at this time. Outdoor residential burning in Zone 1 is also prohibited on a curtailment day. For information about the wood stove change out program please email mikkib@nsaqmdca.gov or call 530-832-0102 ext. 3.
