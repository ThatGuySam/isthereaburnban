## Authority and geography

- DEM or its representative can issue, cancel or suspend forest-land permits. Chapter 12 has exceptions for certain attended fires, authorized fire districts and supervised burns. Under unusual forest-fire hazard conditions, the Governor can close forests by proclamation and DEM can file and announce an emergency fire directive after written notice from the Governor. DEM can also issue and rescind orders on the public reservations it manages. [Forest fires and prevention statute](https://webserver.rilegislature.gov/Statutes/TITLE2/2-12/INDEX.HTM) · [Park rules § 7.15](https://rules.sos.ri.gov/regulations/Part/250-100-00-7)
- Rhode Island's counties are geographic reporting areas. DEM park rules cover sites in all five, including Colt in Bristol, Goddard Memorial in Kent, Fort Adams in Newport, Lincoln Woods in Providence, and Burlingame in Washington. The town rules map Narragansett, North Kingstown and South Kingstown to Washington County. [Census county definitions](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/2010/geo/state-local-geo-guides-2010/rhode-island.html) · [State park directory](https://riparks.ri.gov/find) · [State municipal directory](https://opengov.sos.ri.gov/GOVDirectoryPublic/OfficialSearch?page=IdxMuni&topmenuId=105)
- Air Resources also limits certain facility and business open burning and authorizes written exemptions. Municipalities can impose stricter conditions. Bristol's September 2026 ordinance restricts outdoor fires, with qualifying recreational and cooking exceptions, and bars burning during drought, high wind or a Red Flag Warning. The state water board lists the July 1 Drought Watch. Ask Bristol Fire how it applies its drought trigger before using the watch to infer an active town restriction. Middletown's May 2026 notice had a September 30 end date.
