Garfield County
What does legal burning entail?
  • Legal Burn Permits are required for any open burns year-round in Garfield County pursuant to MCA 7-33-2205. Establishment of fire season -- permit requirements -- reimbursement of costs. which grants individual counties the ability to establish county-specific controlled burning seasons and their respective permitting requirements. If you burn without a permit or during any fire restrictions, you may be charged with a misdemeanor with possible fines pursuant to MCA 7-33-2206 Violations.
  • Fires shall be ignited when weather, wind and other conditions make it safe to do so.
  • You must have enough water, hand tools/equipment and people to keep your fire under control.
  • Burn permit must be accessible at the burn location.
  • Someone must remain with the fire until it is completely out (no smoke or hot embers present).
  • Permission to burn may be revoked at any time.
