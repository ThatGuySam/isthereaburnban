The City of Great Falls allows open burning by permit from March 1st through November 30th, unless open burning restrictions are in place.  Open burning is allowed to take place in select areas of the community.  These area are determined by the Fire Chief or their designee.  The Open burning permit cost is $75 and the inspection cost is $75.  To obtain a burn permit application and set up an inspection come to Fire Station 1 located at 105 9th Street South.  Violations of the Open Burn ordinance will be charged $50 for each violation.
