Fire Burn permits are issued annually from January 01 through December 31st.  Existing burn permits need to be renewed each new calendar year. 

DO NOT create a new permit if you have an existing permit number.

Burn permit applications are available online at https://app.egovmt.com/burnpermit.

You must have a valid burn permit with you at the time of each burn.

There is no burning allowed after sunset.

Refer to your burn permit for further rules and regulations.
