Sanders County

Online Burn Permits are available for Outdoor Burning in Sanders County from May 1st to Sept 30th annually. Permits expire each year but can be renewed starting April 1st for the May - September burn season. October 1 - November 30 is open burning, no permit is required, however you must call the State Air Quality Site 1-800-225-6779. Sanders County is in Airshed 2.
