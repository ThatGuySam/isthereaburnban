A burn ban is in effect for the town of Franklin until further notice.

Fire danger is currently high. Dry leaves on the ground are providing significant fuel promoting rapid spread of wildfires.

Please note that camp fires are permitted, however a camp fire is defined as having a diameter of no more than 3 feet.

Reminder that all outdoor burning requires a permit that can be obtained by calling 285-2050.
