Open Burn Permit Requirements

Open burning is defined as a fire where material is burned on the ground without any type of containment. Brush piles, large bonfires, and uncontained yard debris all require an open burn permit year-round in accordance with Section 7-102 of the Sevierville Municipal Code. Outdoor fireplaces, fire pits, and other forms of containment do not qualify as open burning and do not need a permit. Please call 865-868-1710 if you are unsure if you need a permit.

Permits are available Monday through Friday between 8-5 provided the following requirements are met.

  • An adult (over 18 years of age) is present on site during the burn.
  • Water or other extinguishing materials are available at the site.
  • No gasoline, kerosene, diesel fuel, or flammable solids are to be used as an accelerant.
  • Wind velocity does not exceed five (5) miles per hour.
  • All burning must comply with State of Tennessee Air Quality Law. The issuance of the permit does not relieve the applicant of the necessity to obtain any required State permits.
  • The burning of anything other than leaves, branches, tree limbs, twigs, lawn clippings, woody vegetation, yard trimmings, clean unpainted, uncoated wood or untreated lumber is not permitted.
  • Complaints may void the permit and require extinguishment.
  • Water required to control or extinguish the burn will be billed to the Permit Holder.
