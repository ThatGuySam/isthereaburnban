Dickson Fire Department issues burn permits for burning within the City of Dickson. Permits are issued daily, given that there is no current burn ban. The initial application can be made in person or online. Please submit a request below or call 615-446-6331 for your initial permit. Once the initial application is on file with the fire department, you may call to obtain a burn permit for the day. Burning is limited to natural vegetation grown on your property. Burning of material from another site or burning construction material is strictly prohibited. You are responsible for smoke produced while burning. Please keep fire small enough that ash and debris stay on your property. Permit will be revoked if it becomes a nuisance.
