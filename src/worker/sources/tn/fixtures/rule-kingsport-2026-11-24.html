There is year round burn permit system in the City of Kingsport. Improper outdoor burning can lead to fines up to $25,000 by TDEC.

A burn permit is required for any open burning within the City of Kingsport.

Burn permits are only valid for the specific date listed on the application and be approved in advance; please allow up to three (3) business days for processing.

Applicants must review the City’s Open Burning Guidelines prior to submitting a request to ensure compliance with all local and state regulations.

To obtain a burn permit inside the City of Kingsport click here or got to https://king-trk.aspgov.com/eTRAKiT/

Even though open burning of natural materials such as leaves and brush is permissible inside the City of Kingsport, the Kingsport Fire Marshal’s Office encourages city residents to use the brush pickup service of Kingsport Public Works Streets & Sanitation at 423-229-9451.
