For the City of Manchester residents, you need to call 728-2999 and provide the following information to the Fire Department: -Name, Phone Number, Address and what you are burning. Remember you cannot burn leaves or trash in the City of Manchester only natural wood products, tree limbs etc. For County Residents with a Manchester address you can either call the State Division of Forestry at 877-350-2876 or go to their website (www.burnsafetn.org) if you have a smart phone or computer to obtain your burn permit in the county.
