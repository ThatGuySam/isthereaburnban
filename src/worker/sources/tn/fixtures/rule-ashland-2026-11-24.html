Within Ashland City

If you live in Ashland City you are required to retain a burn permit year round. Burn permits are available from the Ashland City Fire Department:

Online: Online Burn Permits | Ashland City Tennessee (ashlandcitytn.gov)

In-person: Station 1 – 7 days a week, during normal hours.

Get in touch with us if you have any questions about your burn location.

Remember you are only allowed to burn natural vegetation within the Ashland City limits. Burning household trash is not permissible!
