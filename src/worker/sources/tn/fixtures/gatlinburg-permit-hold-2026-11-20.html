GFD suspends burn permits amid High Fire Danger issued by Tennessee Department of Forestry for Sevier County

City of Gatlinburg

November 20, 2026

GATLINBURG, Tenn.—The Tennessee Division of Forestry has issued a High Fire Danger designation for Sevier County through at least Sunday, November 22. As a result of these conditions, burn permits will not be issued by the Gatlinburg Fire Department until conditions improve. The High Fire Danger has been issued as dry conditions, low humidity, and increased winds will create an increased risk for wildland fires.

The City of Gatlinburg strongly encourages residents and visitors to refrain from all outdoor burning during this period. Current weather conditions can cause fires to spread rapidly and become difficult to control, posing a significant threat to lives, property, and surrounding wooded areas.

“With the dry conditions and increased winds we are experiencing, even a small outdoor fire can quickly become dangerous,” Gatlinburg Fire Department interim chief Wayne Brandenburg said. “We ask everyone to take these conditions seriously and help protect our community by avoiding outdoor burning and exercising extreme caution with potential ignition sources.”

The Gatlinburg Fire Department reminds residents and visitors to take the following precautions:

·      Refrain from outdoor burning until conditions improve.
·      Properly extinguish and dispose of cigarettes and matches.
·      Avoid activities that could produce sparks or open flames near dry vegetation.
·      Keep vehicles and equipment off dry grass and other combustible materials.

Immediately report any signs of an uncontrolled fire by calling 911.

The City of Gatlinburg will continue to monitor fire danger conditions and provide updates regarding the issuance of burn permits as conditions warrant.

For additional information regarding burn permits within the City of Gatlinburg, contact the Gatlinburg Fire Department at 865-436-5112.
