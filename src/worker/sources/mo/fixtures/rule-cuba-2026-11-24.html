[CC 1996 § 14-9; Ord. No. 632 § 3]
It shall be unlawful for any person, firm or corporation to dispose of any solid waste by depositing the same in or on any public alley, street, roadway, highway, vacant lot, or property of any kind or character within the City, or in any stream or body of water within the City, or by burning the same (except as herein provided); however, nothing in this Article shall be construed to prohibit the disposition of solid waste in a solid waste container or the disposal of burnable solid waste in an incinerator which is enclosed within a building and approved by the building inspector. Waste paper, paper boxes, leaves and other burnable solid waste may be burned between the hours of 6:00 A.M. and 8:00 P.M. in an outdoor incinerator, metal container or masonry pit approved by the Fire Chief of the City.
