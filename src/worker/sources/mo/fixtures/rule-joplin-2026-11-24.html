City of Joplin Burn Permit Guidelines & Safety Rules

What You Can Burn

  • Allowed: Only residential vegetative material (such as tree branches and leaves).
  • Pile Size: Burn piles must be less than 3 feet in diameter and no more than 2 feet high.
  • Prohibited: Commercial land clearing operations are strictly prohibited by City Ordinance.

Permit Information & Validity

  • Cost & Duration: The burn permit costs $10 and is valid for 3 consecutive days.
  • Weather Extension: If the Fire Department does not allow burning on any of the 3 days covered by your permit, it may be re-issued for another 3-day period. This must be requested within 7 days of the original permit’s expiration.
  • Daily Verification: For your permit to be valid, you must call 417-623-0403 each day to determine if the Fire Department is allowing burning.
  • Enforcement: The valid permit must be kept by the person conducting the burn and presented upon request to Police or Fire officials.

Exceptions (No Permit Required)

Permits are NOT required for outdoor cooking, barbecuing, or smoking fires within approved grills, smokers, spits, pans, chimineas, or outdoor fireplaces. However, these units must be at least 15 feet away from any structure.

Open Burning Safety Rules

  • Timing: No burning is allowed after sunset.
  • Clearance: The burn pile must be at least 50 feet away from any structure.
  • Supervision: A responsible adult must always be present during open burning.
  • Extinguishment: The person attending the fire must have a ready means of extinguishment (garden hose or fire extinguisher) available at the burn location.
  • Complaints: Citizen complaints will be investigated. Illegal burning operations will result in a summons to City Municipal Court.
