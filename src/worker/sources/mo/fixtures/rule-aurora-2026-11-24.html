[Ord. No. 2022-3267, 10-11-2022]
A. 
It shall be unlawful for any person to conduct any open burning within the City, except as permitted by this Chapter.
1. 
Open burning of yard waste is only authorized for yard waste generated upon a landowner's property and at the location where the burning is to take place. Yard waste may not be transported to another location for open burning.
2. 
No burning shall be permitted on City right-of-way except by the City.
3. 
The location for any open burning shall be not less than twenty-five (25) feet from any premises and provisions shall be made to prevent the fire spreading to within twenty-five (25) feet of any premises.
4. 
A yard waste fire shall not be more than five (5) feet by five (5) feet by five (5) feet in dimension and if in a container, not more than can be placed within an upright metal container no larger than fifty-five (55) gallons in capacity.
5. 
Open burning shall not be used for waste disposal purposes or burning of inorganic material such as plastic, tires, rubber or any other material that produces offensive fumes and immense smoke when burned.
6. 
Open burning of yard waste is only permitted from sunrise to sunset.
7. 
All open burning shall be constantly attended by a competent adult person until such open burning is extinguished. This person shall have a garden hose connected to the water supply or other fire extinguishing equipment readily available for use.
8. 
Open burning for commercial land-clearing operations must be preapproved by a permit to open-burn vegetative or untreated wood obtained through the Missouri Department of Natural Resources, and a permit obtained and approved through the City of Aurora Community Development Office and the Aurora Fire Department.
9. 
Open burning shall be allowed without prior notification to the Fire Chief or his/her designee for highway safety flares, railroad smudge pots and similar occupational needs.
10. 
The Fire Chief or his/her designee shall prohibit open burning which will be offensive or objectionable due to smoke or odor emissions when atmospheric conditions or local circumstances make such fires hazardous. The Fire Chief or his/her designee shall order the extinguishment, by the permit holder or the Fire Department, for any open burning, which creates or adds to a hazardous or objectionable situation. Permission for open burning authorizes the City of Aurora Fire Department to come on the premises where the fire is located to extinguish the open burning if necessary.
11. 
Yard waste fires shall be ignited only by a small amount of paper and no flammable liquids will be used to ignite such fires .
12. 
Smoke and odor from the burning of yard waste must not create a nuisance.
