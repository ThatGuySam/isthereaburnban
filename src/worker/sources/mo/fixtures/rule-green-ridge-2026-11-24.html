[CC 2007 § 17.110; Ord. No. 480, 6-10-1996]
A. 
All open burning shall take place at least fifteen (15) feet away from any building or property line. Firefighting material, including, but not limited to, a hose or container of water sufficient to contain any open burning shall be located at the site of the burning. The open burning shall at all times be attended by a person capable of containing the fire, should containment become necessary. If the health or safety of the public may be endangered, the Chief of the Green Ridge Volunteer Fire Department may invoke a temporary burning ban. No open burning shall take place during any time when the Chief of the Pettis County Fire Protection District or Chief of the Green Ridge Volunteer Fire Department has prohibited or banned any open burning within said districts. No open burning shall take place in the public streets, and the area surrounding open fires shall be kept neat and clean, and free from accumulations of trash, refuse and debris at all times.
B. 
Open burning shall otherwise be allowed under the following conditions:
1. 
The materials to be burned must not produce excessive smoke or noxious odors.
