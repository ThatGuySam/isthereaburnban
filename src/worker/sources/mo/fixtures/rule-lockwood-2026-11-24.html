[Ord. No. A-281 §§1 — 2, 7-28-1997; Ord. No. A-347 §1, 7-11-2005]
A. 
Within the corporate limits of the City, the open burning of residential yard waste consisting of leaves and brush from vegetation grown on property is permitted.
1. 
Burning shall only take place from sunrise to sunset except during windy or extremely dry conditions.
2. 
The burning of paper, garbage, tires and other kinds of waste is prohibited.
3. 
No burn barrels allowed at any time.
B. 
Any person violating this Section is guilty of a misdemeanor.
