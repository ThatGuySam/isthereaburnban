[Ord. No. 1942, 7-23-2020; Ord. No. 2189, 8-28-2025]
A. 
Definitions. As used in this Section the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:
APPROVED BURN CONTAINER
A non-combustible container with a screen, (burn barrel, barbeque grill, smoker, or outdoor fireplace) designed to contain a fire. [Minimum of ten (10) feet from any dwelling, building, structure, shed or garage, unless fire barrier materials are in place.]
OPEN BURNING
The burning of leaves, tree branches, yard trimmings, grass clippings or other natural vegetation. [Minimum of twenty-five (25) feet from any dwelling, building, structure, shed, or garage, unless fire barrier materials are in place.]
OUTDOOR FIREPLACE
A solid fuel outdoor burning fireplace that may be constructed of steel, concrete, clay, or other non-combustible material. [Minimum of ten (10) feet from any dwelling, building, structure, shed or garage, unless fire barrier materials are in place.]
RECREATIONAL FIRE
An outdoor fire where the fuel being burned is NOT contained in an incinerator, outdoor fireplace, barbeque grill, smoker, or barbeque pit and has a total fuel area of three (3) feet or less in diameter and two (2) feet or less in height, for pleasure, religious, ceremonial, cooking, warmth or similar purposes. [Minimum of twenty-five (25) feet from any dwelling, building, structure, shed, or garage, unless fire barrier materials are in place.]
SITE CLEARING BURNING
The open burning of trees and vegetation removed during land clearing to be burned on site should occur at least two hundred (200) feet from the nearest property line not owned by the party conducting the open burn. [Approval from the District Fire Marshall or District Chief twenty-four (24) hours prior to ignition.]
B. 
Prohibited Materials. State of Missouri regulations prohibit any waste generated by a business, trade, industry, salvage, or demolition operation unless it is untreated or vegetative wood waste. Wastes that may not be burned, include, but are not limited to, tires, carpet, used oil, asphalt roofing materials, rubber products, hazardous materials, Styrofoam, plastics, petroleum-based products, treated wood, any asbestos-contained products, and any other material deemed hazardous by the Code Official.
C. 
Allowable Materials. Items that are allowed to be burned within the City limits upon applying for and obtaining a burn permit include: cardboard and paper products (in an approved burn container) small limbs, leaves, grass clippings, and garden waste.
D. 
Burn permits shall be issued without charge by the Building Regulations Department. Burn permits can be obtained 24/7 online at www.marshfieldmo.gov and shall be valid not to exceed thirty (30) days. Burn permits will not be issued during times of extreme wind or dry conditions or when the City of Marshfield is under a Red Flag Warning issued by the National Weather Service or when a City-wide burn ban has been established.
E. 
Permits to burn the above allowable materials may be obtained by business owners with the approval of the District Fire Marshal, District Fire Chief, or other designated representative as authorized by the District Fire Chief.
F. 
No open burning or recreation fire shall be started or allowed to continue burning when the wind speed is fifteen (15) miles per hour or greater or when the wind direction or wind speed will cause embers or other burning materials to be carried onto any structure, combustible material or a neighboring property, nor at any time that the direction of the wind will carry smoke into the open windows of any building.
G. 
All open burning and recreation fires shall be constantly attended and supervised by a competent person at least eighteen (18) years of age until the fire has been completely extinguished. The means for extinguishing any fire must be immediately available, including at least one (1), but not limited to, a garden hose connected to a water source, shovels and/or rakes, sand or soil, water buckets, or an ABC-rated fire extinguisher with a capacity of at least ten (10) pounds.
H. 
No fire shall cause a nuisance. Any release of noxious smoke, offensive odor or smoke of sufficient quantity or quality to prohibit the use and enjoyment of neighboring property is hereby declared a nuisance. If a violation is observed, or if a nuisance is created, the District Fire Chief or his/her designated representative has the authority to require it to be extinguished.
I. 
No open burning shall be allowed on any street or alley, except leaves may be burned between October and April in a ditch line; provided, it is ten (10) feet from any combustible culvert. Open burning may only be conducted during daylight hours unless otherwise authorized by the District Fire Marshall, District Fire Chief, or other designated representatives as authorized by the Fire Department.
J. 
No open burning or recreation fires shall be closer than six (6) feet from adjoining property lines or ten (10) feet from any wooden fence, wooden deck, propane or gas tank, or combustible materials.
K. 
Violations and complaints shall be investigated by the District Fire Chief or his/her designated representative. If a violation is observed, or if a nuisance is created, the District Fire Chief or his/her designated representative will order that the fire be extinguished. If the order is not complied with, the Marshfield Fire Protection District will be called to extinguish the fire. Persons found in violation of this Code shall be subject to a fine and/or confinement as provided by Section 100.240.
