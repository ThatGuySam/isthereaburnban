B. 
The following is an addition to the minimum requirements as adopted by the 2018 International Fire Code:
1. 
An automatic smoke detection system shall be installed in ALL occupancies regardless of occupant load or number of stories.
2. 
An automatic smoke detection system will not be required where an automatic sprinkler system and occupant notification system and monitoring service is in place unless required by the minimum requirements of 2018 International Fire Code Sections 907.2 and 907.9.
3. 
An automatic smoke detection system shall be interconnected in new construction and where practical in existing occupancies under major alterations as defined (2018 International Existing Building Code Chapter 6).
4. 
In existing buildings not undergoing alteration, a detector and occupant system can utilize individual detectors or a interconnected system as appropriate to construction restrictions.
5. 
Detection units must be plated in all closed rooms or where a header exceeds more than ten percent (10%) of the wall height.
6. 
Outside monitoring service is required when installed in a multi-residential unit. Commercial structures connected by party wall/shared wall will also be required to utilize a monitoring service. A stand-alone commercial structure not containing life safety hazards or materials will be subject to owner preference.
7. 
Outside monitoring and notification shall be required on commercial kitchen hoods with automatic extinguishing systems.
8. 
Automatic sprinkler systems or an alternative system may be required in commercial structures containing a higher hazard environment as deemed necessary by the Fire Code Official.
C. 
The following is an addition to the minimum requirements as adopted by the 2018 International Fire Code:
1. 
Any commercial operated building with an occupancy (A, B, E, F, H, I, M, R, S, and U) under regulation by the City of Sikeston shall be required to install a Knox Box. This will be accessible to all Sikeston Department of Public Safety officials.
2. 
The key box will not be required on stand-alone buildings where there is no risk of fire impinging on neighboring buildings who are more than thirty (30) feet away. The thirty(30) feet creates a safe distance to guard against wind driven fires.
3. 
The key box will not be required on personal single- or multi-family dwellings.
4. 
Contents of the key box will require a updated key capable of granting access by Sikeston Department of Public Safety officials to the structure or lot and any other buildings or elevators where life safety could be effected.
5. 
The Knox Box needs to be purchased from knoxbox.com as it is a UL 1037 tested product.
6. 
The Knox Box should be mounted in a position approved by the Fire Marshal.
