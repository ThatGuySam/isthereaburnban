City Ordinance does not allow the burning of:

  • Household Waste
  • Vegetation or Yard Waste
  • Petroleum Products
  • Construction or Demolition Materials

City Ordinance allows the burning of:

  • No Permit Required:
    • Residential, commercially manufactured outdoor fireplace or outdoor firepit under certain conditions
  • Permit & Inspection Required:
    • Residential, homemade outdoor recreational burning under limited conditions
  • Permit Required:
    • Ceremonial open burning or ceremonial cooking pit under limited conditions
