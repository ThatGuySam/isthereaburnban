Dyea Flats Road Closure - NPS KLGO

The Dyea Flats Road will remain closed to vehicular traffic over the Thanksgiving holiday weekend due to ongoing culvert replacements. Pedestrian traffic is allowed through the construction zone on Thanksgiving Day, however vehicles should be parked clear of the construction site before the "road closed" sign, as the work crew may still be on site. The road is anticipated to open by Saturday, December 5th.
