• It is unlawful for any person to deposit refuse which may be offensive, noxious or dangerous to the public health on any private property, public ground, alley, street, or areaway or on any other public place within the city limits where it may become dangerous or offensive to the public health. Outside burning of rubbish or other combustible materials shall be restricted to authorization of a special permit by the fire chief. Nothing herein contained shall be construed to prevent a person from removing rubbish from his private property and transporting it to an approved disposal area; provided, that all city regulations relating to the use of disposal area are complied with.
