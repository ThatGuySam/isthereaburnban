A. 
No person shall, except as provided in this chapter, deposit refuse which may become offensive, noxious or dangerous to the public health on any private property, public ground, alley, street or area way, or in any other public place within the city limits.
B. 
Outside burning of rubbish or other combustible materials shall be restricted to authorization or by special permit of the fire chief.
C. 
Nothing contained in this section shall be construed to prevent a person from removing rubbish from his or her private property and transporting it to an approved disposal area; provided, however, that all city regulations relating to the use of the disposal area are complied with by the person.
(Prior code § 7.06.03)
