Due to the extended dry conditions, Union County continues to be under a burn ban as of November 20, 2026.

The Union County Courthouse will be closed on Thursday, November 26 and Friday, November 27, 2026 in observance of the Thanksgiving holiday.

There will be no trash pickup on November 26 and 27, due to the holiday.  Trash pickup will resume on Monday, November 30, 2026.   Trash should be back on the regular schedule by the end of the week.

Have a safe and pleasant holiday.
