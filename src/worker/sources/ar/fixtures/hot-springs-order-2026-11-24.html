Burn Ban in Effect for the City of Hot Springs

Beginning Tuesday, August 4 at 1:30 p.m., a burn ban is in effect for the City of Hot Springs. This ban is based on the potential fire hazard associated with low fuel moisture levels, dry conditions and little to no precipitation in the future forecast. The burn ban prohibits all controlled burning within city limits. All burn permits issued prior to the burn ban have been rescinded; they can be reissued or extended when the ban is lifted. The burn ban will be in effect as long as these conditions are present. Information on burn ban status will be available at 501-321-6977. CAPS is an available option to pick up yard debris like leaves and small limbs. They can be reached at 501-321-6921.
