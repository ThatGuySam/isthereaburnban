**IF AT ANY TIME THE WIND SPEEDS MEET OR EXCEED 10MPH, THE FIRE MUST BE IMMEDIATELY EXTINGUISHED.**

WIND SPEEDS ARE SPORADIC AND OFTEN UNPREDICTABLE. THE RESPONSIBLE PARTY OBTAINING THE PERMIT MUST CONTINUALLY MONITOR WIND CONDITIONS. 
Obtaining a Burn Permit    

1. Ensure that winds are below 10 mph.  If at any times wind speeds meet or exceed 10 mph, burn permits are no longer valid.  

2. Check the Benton County Fire Marshal's website at https://bentoncountyar.gov/fire-marshal/ to confirm there is not a Countywide          Burn Ban. 

3. Read the city's Open Burn Policy First.   

4. Complete the permit here: Burn Permit Form, or by calling dispatch 479-636-1234 or Fire Administration at 479-621-1179.

  • If you wish to burn at a construction site, permits must be obtained by calling the Community Risk Reduction Division at 479-621-1100.

Burn Bans

Burn bans can be issued by the county judge or fire marshal. It is a good idea to make sure there are not additional burn bans from any of these agencies before submitting the Burn Permit Form. Submitting this request does not exempt you from any active burn bans from other agencies.

  • View statewide Burn Bans

Wind Speed Warning

You are responsible for checking wind speeds before burning. Wind speeds must be under 10 miles per hour. Violations are punishable by fines and/or imprisonment.
