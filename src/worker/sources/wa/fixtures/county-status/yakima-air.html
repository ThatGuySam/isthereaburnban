2026 Residential Burning Ends

The residential yard debris burning season closed on October 15 and all 2026 residential burn permits have expired.  Residential yard debris may be burned starting March 15, 2027 (with a new permit).

Invitation to Participate - Wood Stove Replacements

The Yakima Regional Clean Air Agency has received grant funding for the Residential Wood Smoke Reduction program.  If you are a vendor or installer of wood or pellet stoves, ductless systems, or HVAC systems and are interested in participating in the program, please contact Mark Edler at 509-834-2050 ext. 110 or [email protected].

Wood Stove Replacement Program

This program includes rebates up to $4,400, no-cost replacements for qualified low-income households, and payments up to $350 for recycling an old device.  For more information and an application, visit our Woodstove Information page.
