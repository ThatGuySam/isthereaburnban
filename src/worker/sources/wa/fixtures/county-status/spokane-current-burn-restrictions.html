Current restrictions:

There are currently no restrictions in place on outdoor burning or wood heating in Spokane County (incorporated and unincorporated).

However, there are requirements that determine whether or not you can legally have an outdoor fire. For example, if you reside in any of Spokane County’s cities, towns, and UGAs, outdoor burning is limited to recreational fires only and with permission of the property owner.

Requirements for recreational fires and other types of outdoor burning in Spokane County are provided here.

There are also requirements for wood heating throughout Spokane County. These requirements are outlined here.
