From June 1 to Oct. 1, burning yard waste, such as leaves, grass, brush and other yard trimmings, is prohibited in unincorporated Chelan County. People can still have campfires at their homes as long as the fire is in an approved pit or device. Residential recreational campfires are prohibited anually beginning July 1. The use of charcoal briquettes also is prohibited annually beginning July 1. Approved propane-fueled devices are never prohibited.
