"Residential Burning" means the outdoor burning of leaves, clippings, prunings and other yard and gardening natural refuse originating on lands immediately adjacent and in close proximity to a human dwelling and burned on such lands by the property owner or his or her designee. Note: Burning a pile not over 4 foot by 4 foot by 3 feet is allowed, unless prohibited by Lincoln County Fire Districts and/or Washington State during times of high fire danger and/or an air pollution episode. Please contact the Lincoln County Sheriff's Office at 509-725-3501 prior to burning.
