Permits are issued when staff are available at both Central station and the Weirs Beach fire station.  Permits are only issued to property owners 18 years and older, and approved renters in person at the Central and Weirs Beach fire stations with written permission from the property owner. This written permission is valid for the current calendar year and must be renewed each calendar year with property owner signature dated within the current year that a permit is being obtained (i.e. property owner signature must be dated in 2026 to obtain a permit for 2026 calendar year, a new permission form must be signed for 2027 calendar year). Property owners may choose to get their seasonal burn permit online at NH Burn Safe America. There is a $5.50 charge if you purchase your burn permit online.
