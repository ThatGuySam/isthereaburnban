Clear Creek County is not currently under any declared fire restrictions. However, if a Red Flag Warning is issued for any portion of the county, the entire county will be under a temporary Stage 1 Fire Restrictions for the duration of the Red Flag Warning. 

For information regarding Stage 1, Stage 2, or Red Flag Warning Fire Restrictions, please see below. 

Stage 1 Fire Restrictions

Pursuant to Clear Creek County Ordinance No. 20, under Stage 1 Fire Restrictions, the following restrictions are in effect:

Open Fires, except those specified below as "allowed" or those listed as "exempt" in Ordinance No. 20, are prohibited, notwithstanding the issuance of a burn permit.

"Open Fire" means any fire outdoors for any purpose, and includes any activity that produces or maintains, or attempts to produce or maintain, spark, flame, or any known ignition sources of Open Fires. The term Open Fire includes activities indoors that produce outdoor sparks or flames, such as through a chimney or vent. Open Fire also includes the sale, use, and possession of fireworks, including permissible fireworks.

Prohibited activities also include, but are not limited to:

  1. Charcoal, wood or pellet grills, barbecues, or smokers; coal and wood burning stoves; and sheepherder’s stoves.
  2. Bonfires, campfires, or any other type of fires, including within fire rings.
  3. Lighting off fireworks of any kind.
  4. Burning trash, rubbish, weeds, ditches, or crops.
  5. Smoking, except while stationary within an area at least six (6) feet in diameter that is barren and cleared of all combustible material, or within an enclosed vehicle, trailer, or building. 
  6. Disposal of any burning objects outdoors, including any cigarette, cigar, or match.
  7. Shooting (discharging of firearms of any kind), except when engaging in legal hunting activities with a valid hunting license or target shooting within the Clear Creek County Sport Shooting Park, located at 3204 Stanley Rd, Dumont. (The US Forest Service also allows shooting at the Devil’s Nose Target Range off of Hwy 103.) 

The following activities are allowed during Stage 1 Fire Restrictions:

  • Persons using pressurized liquid or gas devices (stoves, grills, lanterns, firepits, or other heating devices) with shut-off valves in an area at least three (3) feet away from any flammable materials.
  • Residents, owners, or lessees who are using a wood stove or other fireplace in a permanent dwelling, provided the chimney is equipped with an effective and properly installed USDA-approved or SAE-approved spark arrestor.
  • Open fires required in religious ceremonies and for which a valid written permit has been granted in advance by the local fire authority or district.
  • Commercial barbecue operations for which a valid written permit has been granted in advance by the local fire authority or district. 

ENFORCEMENT

Violators may be charged with 4th degree Arson, and/or Reckless Endangerment, or unlawful conduct on public or private property, or as provided in Clear Creek County Ordinance No. 20, as determined by the Sheriff.

In addition to other fines, penalties, and legal remedies available by law, any person who violates this Ordinance and causes an Open Fire that requires a response by any first responder agency, including without limitation the Clear Creek Sheriff's Office, Clear Creek EMS, Clear Creek Fire Authority, Evergreen Fire/Rescue, or any other fire protection district or local fire department, shall be responsible for reimbursing each responding agency for its expenses incurred in responding. Restitution, where due, shall be paid to the responding agency.

Stage 2 Fire Restrictions

Pursuant to Clear Creek County Ordinance No. 20, under Stage 2 Fire Restrictions, the following restrictions are in effect:

Open Fires, except those specified below as "allowed" or those listed as "exempt" in Ordinance No. 20, are prohibited, notwithstanding the issuance of a burn permit.

"Open Fire" means any fire outdoors for any purpose, and includes any activity that produces or maintains, or attempts to produce or maintain, spark, flame, or any known ignition sources of Open Fires. The term Open Fire includes activities indoors that produce outdoor sparks or flames, such as through a chimney or vent. Open Fire also includes the sale, use, and possession of fireworks, including permissible fireworks.

Prohibited activities also include, but are not limited to:

  1. Charcoal, wood or pellet grills, barbeques, or smoker; coal and wood burning stoves; and sheepherder's stoves.
  2. Bonfires, campfires, or any other types of fires, including within fire rings.
  3. Burning trash, rubbish, weeds, ditches, or crops.
  4. Propane fire pits or other propane heating devices.
  5. The sale, possession, discharge or use of any type of fireworks or other pyrotechnics device, including sparklers.
  6. Smoking, except while stationary within an area at least ten (10) feet in diameter that is barren and cleared of all combustible material, or within an enclosed vehicle, trailer, or building.
  7. Disposal of any burning object outdoors, including any cigarette, cigar or match.
  8. Possessing or operating off roads any motor vehicle or off-highway vehicle (OHV), regardless of whether the OHV is classified as a motor vehicle. The only exceptions to this prohibition are:
    1. Vehicles may be parked in an area devoid of vegetation within 10 feet of a roadway or a driveway serving private property.
    2. Overnight parking is permitted in Forest Service-developed campgrounds and trailheads.
  9. Shooting (discharging of firearms of any kind), except when engaging in legal hunting activities with a valid hunting license or target shooting within the Clear Creek County Sport Shooting Park, located at 3204 Stanley Road, Dumont, CO 80436.  
  10. Operating a chain saw, generator, or any other device with an internal or external combustion engine without having (a) a USDA or SAE-approved spark arresting device properly installed and in proper working order, (b) an extinguishing source, water, pressurized water extinguisher, or a proper chemical pressurized extinguisher, large enough to handle nearby hazards, and (c) at least one large-size, pointed shovel with an overall length of at least thirty-six inches, readily available for quick use. 
  11. Operating welding and cutting torch appliances, except where the work is (a) conducted entirely indoors, or (b) where the work is conducted outdoors within an area that is barren or cleared of all combustible material at least 30 feet in all directions from the operating equipment and the local Fire Authority or District has issued a valid written hot work permit. 

"Welding and Cutting Torch Appliances" include, but are not limited to: electric, portable and/or gas-fueled devices that generate high-temperature flames, heat, sparks, molten metal slag, or other flammable material typically used to melt, fuse, join, sever or separate metals.

 12. All other outdoor spark- or flame-producing activities.

The following activities are allowed during Stage 2 Fire Restrictions:

  • Propane or natural gas barbeque or liquid fuel stove if attended at all times and in a fully enclosed, covered barbeque grill or contained within a liquid fuel stove.
  • Residents, owners, or lessees who are using a wood stove or other fireplace in a permanent dwelling, provided that the chimney is equipped with an effective and properly installed USDA or SAE-approved spark arrestor.
  • Open fires required in religious ceremonies and for which a valid written permit has been granted in advance by the local Fire Authority or District.
  • Commercial barbeque operations for which a valid written permit has been granted by the local Fire Authority or District.

ENFORCEMENT

Violators may be charged with 4th Degree Arson, and/or Reckless Endangerment, or unlawful conduct on public or private property, or as provided in Clear Creek County Ordinance No. 20, as determined by the Sheriff.  

In addition to other fines, penalties, and legal remedies available by law, any person who violates this Ordinance and causes an Open Fire that requires a response by any first responder agency, including without limitation the Clear Creek Sheriff's Office, Clear Creek EMS, Clear Creek Fire Authority, Evergreen Fire/Rescue, or any other fire protection district or local fire department, shall be responsible for reimbursing each responding agency for its expenses incurred in responding. Restitution, where due, shall be paid to the responding agency.

Additional Fire Restriction Information

Red Flag Warning Fire Restrictions

Red Flag Warnings are issued by the National Weather Service when there is extreme fire danger due to high winds, low relative humidity, and critically dry fuels (vegetation, brush, trees). When the NWS issues a Red Flag Warning for any portion of Clear Creek County, the entire county will go into a temporary Stage 1 Fire Ban. 

During a Red Flag Warning issued for any part of Clear Creek County, the entire county will be under a temporary Stage 1 Fire Ban as follows:

  • The temporary Stage 1 Fire Ban will be in effect for the same time the Red Flag Warning is in effect. For example, if the Red Flag Warning is issued for 11am - 6pm, the Stage 1 Fire Ban will be in effect from 11am - 6pm.
  • Please see the Stage 1 Fire Ban declaration and/or description below for details of activities that are prohibited when any part of Clear Creek County is in a Red Flag Warning.
