Fire Restrictions

Routt County has rescinded all fire restrictions effective at 12:01 AM on Thursday, October 1, 2026. While Routt County has recently received some rain, the entire county is still in a drought status. Fire danger still exists. Please be responsible when having any kind of fire, whether at home or while out recreating. Fires should never be left unattended, and all fires must be cold to the touch before you leave them.

The Routt County Sheriff maintains the authority to enter into Stage 2 Fire Restrictions year-round during periods of High Fire Danger. This is done in accordance with Routt County Ordinance 2026-001. High Fire Danger means the National Weather Service has issued a Red Flag Warning, Fire Weather Watch, High Wind Warning, or a High Wind Watch for or portions of Routt County. All residents and visitors are required to know the current fire restrictions at all times. Details of the Stage 2 Restrictions can be found in the linked ordinance above.
