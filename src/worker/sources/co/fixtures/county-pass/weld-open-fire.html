If you are seeking to conduct a non-exempt open burn in Weld County, then you require either a general Open Burn Permit, issued through the Weld County Department of Public Health and Environment, or a Smoke Management Permit issued by the State of Colorado Air Pollution Control Division. If you determine the burn you are planning to conduct is non-exempt, continue to the “How do I obtain a burn permit” section. See list of exemptions below.
