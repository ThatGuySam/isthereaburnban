1. Important : Before you burn - Airshed 8 (Utah County) is required to have a clearing index of 500 or higher for burning conditions to be favorable and for Open Burning to be allowed.

  2. Residents of cities must have a burn permit available at https://air.utah.gov/OpenBurning/form/index.php

    Residents of Mapleton, Orem, Provo and Springville must contact their City or Fire Chief.

  3. Residents in Unincorporated Utah County must have a valid burn permit from the Utah County Fire Marshal.
