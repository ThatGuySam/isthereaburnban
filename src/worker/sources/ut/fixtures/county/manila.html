Current Fire and Firework Restrictions for the Town of Manila

The Town of Manila has lifted the Stage 2 fire restrictions, effective August 21, 2026. Residential fire pits within Town limits are now allowed, provided the property has running water. Residents are reminded to use all fires safely and responsibly and to comply with applicable fire-safety requirements.

Stage 2 Fire Restrictions Lifted in the Town of Manila

Effective August 21, 2026

The Town of Manila is pleased to announce that Stage 2 Fire Restrictions within the Town of Manila have been lifted, effective August 21, 2026.

With the lifting of Stage 2 restrictions, residents may once again use residential fire pits within the Town limits, provided running water is available at the property. 

What This Means for Manila Residents

Beginning August 21, 2026:

  • Residential backyard fire pits within the Town of Manila are allowed when the property has running water.

  • Residents are still encouraged to use extreme caution and practice responsible fire safety.

  • Never leave a fire unattended, and always make sure a fire is completely extinguished before leaving it.

Important Information for Surrounding Public Lands

Fire restrictions are determined by the individual land-management agency and may differ outside the Town of Manila.

The Bureau of Land Management (BLM) is expected to rescind its Stage 2 order on BLM-managed lands. However, residents and visitors should verify the current restrictions before having a fire on BLM property.

Tribal lands and National Park Service lands will remain under Stage 2 fire restrictions at this time.

The Ashley National Forest currently has no fire restrictions.

Please remember that the lifting of restrictions within the Town of Manila does not automatically the State of Utah lift restrictions on surrounding federal, tribal, or National Park Service lands.

The Town encourages everyone to continue practicing good fire safety and to check current restrictions before traveling outside Town limits.

Thank You!

We appreciate our residents' cooperation throughout the fire restriction period. Your efforts helped protect our community.

Thank you for helping keep the Town of Manila safe!

For questions regarding fire restrictions within the Town of Manila, please contact the Town of Manila Office at 435-784-3143.

For the most current statewide fire restriction information, please refer to the Utah Department of Natural Resources, Division of Forestry, Fire and State Lands, and the appropriate land-management agency for the area you are visiting.

If your plans include the Ashley National Forest or surrounding mountain areas, we strongly recommend checking the current restrictions before your trip. For the most up-to-date information regarding the Flaming Gorge Ranger District, please contact their Manila office at (435) 784-3445.

With continued cooler, wet weather and changing fire conditions, restrictions may be updated with little notice. We encourage all residents and visitors to stay informed by monitoring current state and federal fire restrictions before recreating outdoors.

Water Summer Usage and Restrictions:

On May 28, 2026, the Town Council adopted Ordinance No. 05-28-26b, establishing water conservation measures, including outdoor watering days and times.

Section 3. Water Use Restrictions

Outdoor Watering Schedule

To help conserve water and reduce evaporation during the hottest part of the day, all outdoor watering of lawns, gardens, and landscaping is limited as follows:

  • Permitted Watering Days: No more than three (3) days per week per property.
    • Properties with even-numbered addresses may water on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays.
    • Properties with odd-numbered addresses may water on Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Saturdays.
  • No watering is permitted on Sundays to allow our aquifers to replenish.

Permitted Watering Hours:

  • Before 10:00 a.m.
  • After 6:00 p.m.

No watering is allowed between 10:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m.

These restrictions reflect best management practices designed to reduce water loss through evaporation and promote responsible water use throughout the community.

Please note: Violations of this ordinance may result in penalties.
