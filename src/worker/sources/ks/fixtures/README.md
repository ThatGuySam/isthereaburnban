## Rules and current status

- K.A.R. 28-19-645 generally prohibits open burning except under K.A.R. 28-19-647 and 28-19-648. Johnson County, KDHE's designated local air-quality agent, publishes the applicable exemptions and prior-approval categories. The EPA publishes the general rule and exceptions. K.A.R. 28-19-645a supersedes the general rule in the 16 named Central Time counties throughout April. The Kansas Forest Service publishes the county table, and its KDHE regulation PDF confirms the full text and the exact scope. The November 24 publication uses the general rule in all 105 counties. [State regulations PDF at KS Fire](https://ksfire.org/media/OPEN_BURNING_RESTRICTIONSrev_April_2012-b451b87.pdf) · [EPA rule index](https://www.epa.gov/air-quality-implementation-plans/kansas-sip-open-burning-restrictions) · [Johnson County open-burning program](https://www.jocogov.org/department/environment/air-quality/open-burning)
- Dickinson's GIS page identifies the county's ArcGIS WebMap as the operational map for controlled burning. Resolution 040623 gives local fire chiefs or designees authority to direct dispatch to suspend and restore permits in their jurisdictions, including a limited grassland option. The layer subtype codes are 1 for No Ban, 2 for Banned and 3 for Grassland Only. The WebMap renderer spells the third label “Gassland Only,” while the layer's subtype and county wording agree on its meaning. Use the layer types. [Dickinson GIS](https://www.dkcoks.gov/Maps-and-Data) · [Public web map](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=9d6edf18c4dc4a5eb5c6a15f9bac5cdd) · [County resolution and permits](https://www.dkcoks.gov/Controlled-Burns)
- Both Dickinson responses had `Last-Modified: Mon, 23 Nov 2026 16:24:12 GMT`, consistent with the layer's `dataLastEditDate`. There is no edit or effective date for a district row. The parser uses the layer date as a publication timestamp and drops it after seven days without a layer edit. The 12 fire district polygons intersect Dickinson County and some extend beyond it. Only the areas within Dickinson are mapped. Four districts showed code 2 on November 24. They are Longford–Talmage, Dickinson County District 1, Saline County District 1 and Enterprise. The other eight showed code 1, and no countywide all-clear follows from those rows. [Dickinson County map](https://www.dkcoks.gov/Burn-Restrictions-Map)
