Permitted Open Burns

The open burn permit application is available for the following open burns:

  • Bon Fire (permit not required, but application is requested)
  • Land Clearing and Property Maintenance (90 day permit)
  • Tree and Garden Trimmings (30 day permit)

Bon Fire Permit Requirements

If the bon fire applicant is not the owner of property, the owner's written permission shall be obtained. A principal is acceptable for school property.

  • Pile Location: All open burns must be 50 feet from any structure or combustible materials.
  • Pile Size: Bonfire piles shall not exceed 5 feet (width), 5 feet (depth) and 5 feet (height).
  • Pile Materials: Only clean wood or firewood is allowed to be used as fuel.

Land Clearing and Property Maintenance Permit Requirements

A land clearing and property maintenance permit application must include: 1. payment of the $400 fee, 2. certificate of liability insurance coverage (general liability coverage needed at all times while burning; liability insurance minimum amount of $1,000,000), 3. land development permit ID number and 4. site plan of burn site.

  • Pile Location: All open burns must be 50 feet from any structure or combustible materials. Land clearing and property maintenance piles must be a minimum of 500 feet from occupied building unless prior permission from neighbors is given using the neighbor permission form (PDF).
  • Pile Size: Land clearing and property maintenance piles shall not exceed 25 feet (width), 25 feet (depth) and 15 feet (height). Burning must take place on site where debris was generated.
  • Pile Materials: Any debris generated from development of property. This includes the removal of unwanted, leaning, hazardous or dead trees, regardless of size, during normal upkeep or landscaping of the property. This could also include, but is not limited to, limbs, tree trunks, logs and stumps.

Tree and Garden Trimmings Permit Requirements

Limited to two tree and garden trimmings permits per burn season per address.

  • Pile Location: All open burns must be 50 feet from any structure or combustible materials. Tree and garden trimmings piles must be a minimum of 300 feet from occupied building unless prior permission from neighbors is given using the neighbor permission form (PDF).
  • Pile Size: Tree and garden trimmings piles shall not exceed 8 feet (width), 6 feet (depth) and 4 feet (height). Burning must take place on premises of private property where trimmings were generated.
  • Pile Materials: Any vegetation that: is commonly removed from trees, shrubs or garden plants during the normal pruning process; falls from trees during normal thunderstorms or windy days; or is removed from a garden after the growing season (corn stalks, tomato vines, bean vines, etc.).
