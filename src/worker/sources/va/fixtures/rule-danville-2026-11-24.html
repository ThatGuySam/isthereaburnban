Regulations for Open Burning Permit

Open burning for disposal of land clearing material or modification of roads and highways, parking areas, buildings, etc. requires a permit from the Fire Marshal, with regulations being as follows:

  • Burning is to be conducted in accordance with all applicable state and local regulations/ordinances. Violation of such regulations/ordinances may be sufficient to cause permit revocation and possible fines.
  • Complaints of smoke or ash nuisance may result in immediate revocation of the permit. The individual, business or contractor to whom this permit is issued is responsible for any damage and/or cleanup.
  • The burning shall be at least 500 feet from any occupied building, excluding buildings located on the same property.
  • The material to be burned shall consist of brush, clean stumps, and similar land-clearing debris and shall not include demolition material, garbage or other waste disposal. The fire official must approve the number and size of debris piles (40 feet by 40 feet maximum) and all piles shall be seasoned for a minimum of fifteen (15) days before burning. The highest possible level of heat is to be maintained to minimize particulate being emitted into the atmosphere.
  • Burning will only be allowed when the relative humidity is 60% or less and the prevailing winds are away from any highway, neighborhood or built-up area.
  • The burning shall be attended at all times. Attendant must be capable of operating on-site land-clearing equipment.
  • Prior notification to those in the general vicinity must be made to mitigate concerns.
  • No material may be hauled from another site and burned.
  • The permit must be kept on the burn site.
  • The fire official may assign additional requirements.

By completing an Open Burning Permit Application Form you are indicating that you have read and understand Danville's Open Burning Regulations. The completed form is to be submitted to the Fire Marshal's Office, 297 Bridge Street, Danville, VA 24541. Questions may be directed to their office at 434-799-5226 or by email.
