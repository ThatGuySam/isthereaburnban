Current Fire Restrictions

As of September 21, 2026 the Temporary Fire Ban has been officially lifted. While outdoor burning with permit and campfires are once again permitted, community safety must remain our top priority. Weather and environmental conditions can change rapidly, and the risk of accidental wildfires remains a persistent threat. Thank you for your continued cooperation in protecting our local forests, homes, and neighbors.


IMPORTANT NOTICE 7/1/2025:

Do you love our beautiful town? So do we! To help protect it, and everything we love about our mountain town, fireworks are not allowed within the Village of Angel Fire at anytime. Even in normal conditions, fireworks carry a real risk of starting wildfires. It only takes one spark to change everything. AFFD appreciates you skipping the fireworks this season, you’re helping keep our forests green, our skies clear, and our community safe.
