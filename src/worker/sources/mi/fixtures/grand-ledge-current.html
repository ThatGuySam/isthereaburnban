PUBLIC NOTICE: Odd-Even, On-Street, Overnight Seasonal Street Parking Reminder

*Public Notices Posted on November 17, 2026 | Last Updated on November 17, 2026

PUBLIC NOTICE: Beginning on Tuesday, December 1, 2026, the city's Odd-Even, Overnight, On-Street Parking will be in effect through March 31, 2027.


The city allows for overnight parking on only one side of the street from December through March, and during any declared snow emergencies. Overnight parking during this time is allowed with alternating, odd/even, sides of the street to better enable snowplowing.

For more information, please visit the city's webpage dedicated to adhering to City Code Section 41-1-42-4, Article I, by clicking here.  


For access to the full 2026-2027 Odd-Even, Overnight, On-Street Parking calendar, click here.

 

Thank you for your cooperation which helps to keep our city streets safe!
