Important Information For Midland County Residents - Burn Permit Procedure

If you choose to burn leaves, brush or yard waste you must have a permit from the Michigan Department of Natural Resources. Midland County residents may receive a burning permit by visiting the Burn Permits link on the right side of this page.

There is no burning allowed in the Village of Sanford or within 1400 feet of the City of Midland. When fire danger is high no burning permits will be issued. Other inquiries to the MDNR can be made by calling (989) 687-7771.
