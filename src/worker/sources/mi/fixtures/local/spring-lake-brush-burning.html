There are several ways to connect with Township services without leaving your home. Here are some of the most frequently asked questions regarding Township and other community services. View a question to learn more.

Pay My Bills and Property Tax

  1. Apply for ...


    Building Permits
    Building permits, electrical permits, mechanical permits, and plumbing permits are handled by Michigan Township Services Muskegon. Permit application forms can be found on the MTSM website.

    Burn Permits
    Burning of brush is allowed on the third Saturday of March, May, July, September and November. A Burn Permit is required, and is valid for a calendar year. Leaves may not be burned. No burning is allowed within 1400 feet of the City of Ferrysburg or the Village of Spring Lake.
  2. Short Term Rental Permits
    Short term rentals are only allowed in the overlay district.
  3. Zoning Permits
    Zoning Permits are issued by Spring Lake Township. Find more information and application forms on the Building & Planning page.

  22. A Complaint Regarding My Cable Provider
    An informal video/cable television complaint can be sent to the Federal Communications Commission by completing the form on the Commission's website, calling the Commission's Video Franchise toll-free number (1-800-292-9555), faxing the complaint to 517-284-8200 or mailing a letter to the Commission.
  23. A Freedom of Information Request
    The Freedom of Information Act is a Michigan Statue that gives the public the right to documents regarding the affairs of government, public officials and public employees. Under the Act, a person has the right to inspect, copy or receive copies of public records of a public body. Learn how to make a Freedom of Information Request.
