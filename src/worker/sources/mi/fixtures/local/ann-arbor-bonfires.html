A bonfire permit is required when a resident or organization would like to have a fire on the ground that is larger than 3 x 3 feet in size. Anything​ smaller is considered a recreation fire and a permit is not required. Bonfires shall not exceed 5 x 5 feet in size and shall not be​ within 50 feet of a structure or materials that burn easily. Fires may not be lit under conditions which may cause the fire to spread within 50 feet of a structure.
