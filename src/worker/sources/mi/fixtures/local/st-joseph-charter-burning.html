Burn permits

In the township and in the Village of Shoreham, any fire larger than 3 feet requires a burn permit issued by the Fire Chief or the Chief's designee.

In the Village of Shoreham, burning on a Sunday also needs a special use permit from the Village Council, and the hours and wind limits above always apply.
