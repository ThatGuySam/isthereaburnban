Burn permit regulations:

  • Be advised that the last day of burning has to be done by midnight.
  • Do not let the fire get too big.
  • Do not burn if it is too windy.
  • Do not leave the fire unattended.
  • NO Building Materials


ATTN:  Burn permits are only good for 3 days only!!  The permit begins on the day you call it in and then the 2 days following the start date of the burn permit.
