*Village Ordinance Sec 30-28: No person shall burn or cause to be burned or authorize the burning of any leaves within the village of Constantine, whether on public or private premises, between October 15 and November 25 of each year. Between November 25 and October 15 of each year burning of leaves within the village will be allowed only between the hours of 12:00 noon and 6:00 p.m. Burning of leaves after 6:00 p.m. or before 12:00 noon shall be considered a violation of this article during this period of time.
