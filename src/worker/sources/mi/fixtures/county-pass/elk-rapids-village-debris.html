Yard Debris​ may be burned during times when the Mi. D N R allows outside burning. You may burn natural debris only, it must be supervised at all times, and you are responsible for any damage you may cause. 

Find out if burning is currently allowed by viewing the DNR burn permit website: 
​www.dnr.state.mi.us/burnpermits/

Village residents: no leaf burning is allowed, trash or burn barrels are not allowed, and you must also obtain a burning permit from the village office in addition to following Mi. D N R rules to burn yard debris. ​​
