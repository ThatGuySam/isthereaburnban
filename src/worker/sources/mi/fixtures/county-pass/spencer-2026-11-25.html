BURN PERMITS

Burn ban in effect starting July 14, 2026 until further notice.  No burn permits will be issued. 
We ask those who decide to do a recreational or campfire, to keep it small, keep ground wet around the area and have a water source available at the base of your fire.  ALWAYS, put fire completely out if you are going to leave it unattended. 
Check back daily for updates.  We need a significant amount of rain, before the ban will be lifted.

For complete Burn Ordinance and rules or to submit your Burn Permit ONLINE click here: https://www.spencerfire.net/burn-ordinance-permits

Or Email burn permit requests to SpencerBurnPermits@gmail.com or call 616-984-2200 and leave a message.
