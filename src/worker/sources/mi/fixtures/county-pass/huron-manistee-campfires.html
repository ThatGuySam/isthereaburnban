ORDER OF THE FOREST SUPERVISOR RESTRICTING THE OCCUPANCY AND USE ON THE HURON-MANISTEE NATIONAL FORESTS

Under the authority of the Act of Congress of June 4, 1897, as amended (16 USC 551), and the Secretary of Agriculture’s Regulations 36 CFR 261.50(a) and or (b), the following acts and omissions are prohibited on the Huron-Manistee National Forests. 

A. CAMPING IN DEVELOPED RECREATION AREAS 

  1. Camping within any one developed campground for a period longer than 14 consecutive days within a 30 day period. After this period the camping equipment and all persons must move from the campground. 36 CFR 261.58(a) 
  2. Entering or being in a developed recreation site or portion thereof when posted CLOSED. 36 CFR 261.58(b) 
  3. Entering or remaining in a campground between 10 PM and 6 AM when posted – except for properly registered campers. 36 CFR 261.58(c) 
  4. Using a developed campsite by more than eight (8) people on a single unit site or by more than sixteen (16) people on a double site. 36 CFR 261.58(f) 
  5. Parking or leaving a vehicle in violation of posted instructions, including the use of a handicap-parking site without a valid state permit properly displayed. 36 CFR 261.58(g) 
  6. Parking a vehicle outside the parking space assigned to one’s own camp unit or other designated parking area. 36 CFR 261.58(h) 
  7. Occupying a developed campsite by parking more than one camper, camp trailer, or motor home (RV) on a single unit site. Parking more than two camping vehicles or camping trailers on a double unit site or exceeding limits to group sites unless otherwise posted. 36 CFR 261.58(d)
  8. Parking, possessing, or leaving more than two vehicles per single unit camp site or four vehicles per double unit sites, except bicycles, motorcycles, or authorized ATVs, unless otherwise posted. 36 CFR 261.58(i) 
  9. Violate posted regulations and instructions. 36 CFR 261.58(b) and 36 CFR 261.53(e) 

B. CAMPING IN DISPERSED AREAS: 

  1. Occupying the same location for more than sixteen (16) consecutive days within a 30 day period. After this period the camping equipment and all persons must move off the National Forest for two weeks. 36 CFR 261.58(a) 
  2. Possessing vehicles, campers, motor homes and trailers without a valid license plate. 36 CFR 261.58(e) 
  3. Leaving a camper and/or camping equipment may be left unattended for more than 24 hours. 36 CFR 261.58(e) 
  4. Camping within two hundred (200) feet of any body of water, except at designated sites. 36 CFR 261.58(e) 
  5. Camping in an area which is posted closed and/or “no camping”. 36 CFR 261.58(e) 
  6. Building, maintaining, attending or using a fire or campfire within 200 feet of a body of water, or designated trail, except at designated sites. 36 CFR 261.52(a)
  7. Violate posted regulations and instructions. 36 CFR 261.58(b) and 36 CFR 261.53(e) 

C. BEING PUBLICLY NUDE. 36 CFR 261.58(j) 

D. WATERCRAFT PROHIBITIONS 

  1. Storing or leaving a watercraft on National Forest land for more than 24 hours. 36 CFR 261.58(p) 
  2. Entering a body of water, operating, riding in, riding on, or being towed by a watercraft in violation of any or all State of Michigan statutes pertaining to personal flotation devices is prohibited. 36 CFR 261.58(k) 
  3. Operating a motorboat without a valid State registration is prohibited. 36 CFR 261.58(n) 
  4. Operate watercraft in violation of any or all State of Michigan and/or Coast Guard regulations. 36 CFR 261.58 (n) 

E. PARKING 

  1. Parking in violation of posted instructions. 36 CFR 261.58(g) 

F. SANITATION 

  1. Possessing, storing or transporting foam coolers, foam beverage and/or foam food containers within 200 feet of a body of water except at designated sites. 36 CFR261.58(cc) 
  2. Possess, store or transport glass beverage within 200 feet of a body of water, except at designated sites. 36 CFR 261.58(cc) 

 

This Order applies to all lands of the Huron-Manistee National Forests. 

Definitions for the purpose of this order: 

Camping means the temporary use of National Forest System lands for the purpose of overnight occupancy without a permanently fixed structure for a short period of time so as not to be considered as residential occupancy by just means of relocating camping equipment from one area to another under the disguise of camping. 

Camping equipment means the personal property used in or suitable for camping, and includes any vehicle used for transportation and all tents, homemade structures or equipment adapted for that use in possession of a person camping. Food and beverage are not considered camping equipment. 

Developed recreation site means an area that has been improved or developed for recreation. 

Designated trail means a trail wholly or partly within or adjacent to and serving the National Forest System that the Forest Service determines is necessary for the protection, administration, and utilization of the National Forest System and the use and development of its resources. 

Vehicle means any device in, upon, or by which any person or property is or may be transported, including any frame, chassis, or body of any motor vehicle, except devices used exclusively upon stationary rails or tracks. 

Pursuant to 36 CFR 261.50 (e) the following persons will be exempt from the order: 

  1. Any Federal, State, or Local officers, or members of an organized rescue or firefighting force engaged in the performance of an official duty. 
  2. Persons with a valid permit in their possession signed by the District Ranger or Forest Supervisor specifically authorizing the otherwise prohibited act or omission. 
  3. Individuals identified as exempt in other agreements or (MOU’s) Memorandum of Understanding. 

Violations of these prohibitions are punishable by a fine of not more than $5000 for an individual or $10,000 for an organization, or imprisonment for not more than 6 months, or both. (16 U.S.C. 551 and 18 U.S.C. 3559 and 3571).
