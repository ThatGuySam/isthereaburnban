Vernon Township

Submit a requrest for a burning permit by completing this online form. In your message, describe in details what, why, and where you will be burning. 

BURN BAN

The Vernon Township Fire Chief has issued a burn ban effective July 13, 2026, and it will remain in place until further notice. All open burning—including brush, yard waste, and recreational fires—is prohibited during this period.

This action is being taken to protect residents and property due to current fire‑risk conditions.

For ongoing updates and the official announcement lifting the ban, please visit the Vernon Township website regularly.
