1. Domestic fires may be permitted in all areas of Benton Charter Township zoned A-I or A-2 any time during the calendar year between the house of sunrise and sunset. Domestic fires are defined as “The burning of yard waste only”
  2. Open burning is allowed in areas zoned Residential; 1 or 2 and Agricultural: Al or A2. A burning permit is required for all open burning.
  3. The burning permit applicant must come to the fire department to properly complete a burning permit application.
  4. Requests for burning permits are received at the fire department; 4713 Hartel Rd. Monday through Friday between the hours of Sam and 5pm, excluding weekends and holidays.
  5. Permits are issued on an annual basis. The resident is responsible to provide the burning permit for inspection and/ or surrender upon request of the Benton Township Fire Department.
  6. YOU must be physically in attendance of the fire at all times. The permit is valid from sunrise to sunset.
  7. YOU must burn on the property the permit is issued to and at least 5 feet from the property line and a minimum of 25 feet from any structure of combustible materials.
  8. YOU shall not use any petroleum products to start the fire or to keep it going.
  9. YOU shall burn only what grows on the property that the permit has been issued to. (Yard Waste)
  10. A source of water must be near the fire at all times.
  11. The brush shall be smaller than 3 inches in diameter. NO STUMPS, NO LOGS, NO BUILDING MATERIALS SHALL BE BURNED.
  12. The brush pile cannot exceed an area of 4 feet wide by 4 feet long and 4 feet high. (4x4x4)
  13. Smoke from the fire must not be offensive to you neighbors.
