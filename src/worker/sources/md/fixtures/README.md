The Forest Service guidance matches COMAR 08.07.04.01–.03 for the woodland scope, exceptions and conditions. Baltimore City is omitted from the woodland mapping because the rule excludes incorporated towns and the DNR page does not establish its applicability there. Burning in the covered areas remains subject to firebreak, staffing and attendance requirements during the ordinary evening hours. The Code of Maryland Regulations viewer directs automated users to its bulk downloads. The summer fixture comes from that publication and defines a June 1 through August 31 rule in 11 counties and Baltimore City.
