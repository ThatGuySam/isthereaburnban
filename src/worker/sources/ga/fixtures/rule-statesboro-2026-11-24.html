The City of Statesboro prohibits outdoor burning without a permit.

There is no charge for a burn permit.

- Burning is limited to yard debris only (i.e., leaves, pine straw, and fallen dead limbs) from 10 AM to 6 PM or at sundown (whichever comes first). 
- All fires must be extinguished and completely out by 6 PM or by dark. 
- Examples of illegal burning include construction materials and garbage. 
- Always have a working water hose nearby. 
- Fires must be 50 feet from all structures and 10 feet from property lines. 

If you live inside the City Limits of Statesboro, you must fill out the form below OR contact the Fire Department at (912)-764-3473 to request a permit.
Please state the location and materials to be burned when calling. Someone from the Fire Department will come to the location and determine if a permit can be issued.
