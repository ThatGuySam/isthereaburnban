What are the laws regarding burning leaves or trash inside the city limits?


​The State of Georgia has restrictions on open burning that can be viewed at the following links:

Open Burning Rules and What You Can Burn

​Furthermore, Crisp County has additional restrictions:

  • No Summer Burning (May 1-September 30)
  • Burn Permit Required
  • (Online: http://www.gatrees.org/OnlinePermits/  
  • By Phone: 1-877-OK2-BURN
  • Fires should not be initiated before 8 AM and should be completely extinguished before dark
