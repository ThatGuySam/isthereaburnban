Burn Ban In Effect

A Burn Ban is currently in effect. The following is prohibited:

  • Seasonal backyard burning  (branches, yard debris, etc.)
  • Commercial agricultural burning (agricultural waste, crops, field burning, etc.)
  • Any other land clearing (slash, stump or controlled forestry burning.)

No Burn Ban In Effect

There is no Burn Ban in effect at this time. Please refer to the resources on this page for safe outdoor burning.
