Archived - Burn Ban

Safety Strategies Posted on March 14, 2025

The original article was published from 3/14/2025 12:00:00 AM to 3/21/2025 11:59:00 PM

A burn ban is in place for the Village of Menomonee Falls.
Due to the Wisconsin DNR's elevated ("very high") fire danger designation in Waukesha County, the Fire Chief has instituted a burn ban for the Village Menomonee Falls.
Several vegetation fires have already been reported throughout the state. This ban will be in place until further notice - we will monitor conditions and follow the DNR's recommendations.
You can follow conditions through the DNR's website:
https://apps.dnr.wi.gov/wisburn/#/
