Irrespective of whether or not Wis. Adm. Code § NR 429.04 is being complied with, it shall be unlawful for any person to burn any materials out of doors within the boundaries of the Town without, at least two hours prior to setting or starting such burning, contacting the Washington County Sheriff's Department and the Fire Department contracted for servicing the location at which such burning is being conducted, informing both departments of the following:
A. 
The exact location and address of the burning.
B. 
The date and time at which the burning is to commence.
C. 
The expected duration of the burning.
D. 
The name of the owner of the property on which the burning is taking place.
E. 
The name and address of the person or persons conducting the burning and the telephone number where such person or persons can be contacted while the burning is being conducted.
