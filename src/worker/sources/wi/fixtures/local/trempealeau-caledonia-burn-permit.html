Burn Permits

Fill out the form below to apply for a burn permit. Once the form is complete you are allowed to burn. As a reminder, you NEED to call Trempealeau County Dispatch prior to igniting your burn and after the burn is completed.

Please do not call the Fire Station for a permit, the phone is not monitored 24/7.
