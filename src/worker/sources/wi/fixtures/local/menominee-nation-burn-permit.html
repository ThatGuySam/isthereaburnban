A. 
Within the exterior boundaries of the Menominee Nation, it shall be unlawful for any person to set any fire unless such fire is attended at all times and the person has first obtained a burning permit on forms supplied by the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources from a duly appointed and designated Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources forest ranger or fire warden, or other person designated by the Tribal Legislature, except:
[Amended 7-12-1990]
(1) 
When a fire is set and designed solely for warming the person or cooking food; or
(2) 
When the ground is snow covered.
B. 
Any person who sets a fire solely for warming the person or cooking food shall extinguish such fire before leaving it and upon failure to do so will be in violation of this chapter.
