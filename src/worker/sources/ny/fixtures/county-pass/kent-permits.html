A Restricted Burn Permit is required for any open burning within the Town of Kent.

(Note: No Open Burning is allowed in the Lake Carmel Sanitation District.)

To obtain a Restricted Burn Permit, please visit the Town of Kent Clerk's Office during normal business hours, or you may print the application (found on the left side of this webpage), sign it, and email it to the Town of Kent Fire Inspector.

Restricted Burn Permits are valid for 90 days from the date of issuance, and there is currently no fee associated with them.

Restricted Burn Permit Conditions

You must call the Kent Police Department at 845-225-5646 and the Putnam County 911 Dispatch Center at 845-225-4860 before any Open Burning.

The use of a Burn Barrel is strictly prohibited and is no longer allowed.

There is no open burning allowed in the entire Lake Carmel Sanitation District. Grass and leaf bags may be available at the Sanitation Department located at 62 Ludington Court, or call 845-225-7030.

There is no burning allowed on Sundays or Holidays.

No burning of any leaves

No burning of any treated wood, such as varnished, stained, sealed, glued, pressure-treated, particle board, fiberboard, or oriented strand board (OSB).

No burning of any trash, refuse, tires, or other solid waste.

No burning of any branch larger than (6) inches in diameter and less than (8) feet long.

Open Burning is allowed from sunrise to sunset, and with weather and climate conditions.

Open burning areas must have a (3) foot clearance, free of any vegetation, and be at least (50) feet from any structure.

You must be present at all times during the Open Burn.

You must have water, a garden hose, and small hand tools (shovel, rake, etc.) present during the Open Burn.

Fire Pits for recreational uses, i.e., cooking or campfire fire do not require a Restricted Burn Permit but must also follow the following restrictions:

They must have a (3) foot clearance, free of any vegetation, and be at least (25) feet from any structure.

You must be present at b during the use of a fire pit.

You must have water, a garden hose, or a fire extinguisher present during the use of a fire pit.

At No Time Should You Ever Use Any Accelerant When Conducting an Open Burn or When Using a Fire Pit (This Includes but Is Not Limited to Kerosene, Gasoline, or Lighter Fluid).

Any violations of any of the restrictions noted above may result in the revocation of the Restricted Burn Permit and issuance of a Town or State Code Violation.

If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact the Kent Fire Inspector via email.

Please check, test, and change batteries in your smoke and carbon monoxide detectors (Alarms)
