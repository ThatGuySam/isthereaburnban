Residential Burn Season is open November 1st - May 15th annually.

DO’s for Residential Burning:

  • Burning is allowed between 08:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. on weekdays, and 08:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. on weekends
  • Burning must be constantly attended by an adult, with a water hose nearby at all times
  • Burn pile sizes must be no larger than 3 ’ x 6 ’ x 3", and you are only allowed to burn one pile at a time. The burn pile must be at least 20' from the nearest structure.
  • Yard waste burning is restricted to natural vegetation generated only on your property and includes grass, tree limbs, branches, leaves, tumbleweeds, pine cones, and similar items.
  • The burn must be 25 feet from any structure.

DON’Ts for Residential Burning:

  • Burning any trash or waste materials, in any quantity, is strictly prohibited. It is unlawful to burn any combustible and noncombustible waste materials such as paper, rags, cartons, tin cans, metals, mineral matter, food, garbage, construction debris, discarded furniture, and tires.
  • No accelerants may be utilized to start a fire. Prohibited accelerants include gasoline, diesel fuel, and any other flammable liquids.
  • No burning is allowed when the winds exceed 5 mph or on legal holidays.

To apply for a burn permit:

Burn Permits are issued during regular business hours, Monday - Friday, 8 AM - 4:30 PM 

  • PVFR Burn Permit Application
  • You must own the property and provide a copy of your Homeowners Insurance (Declarations page) and a copy of your driver's license.
  • Before burning, you must call in and leave a message with your permit number, property address, and type of burn. Please check the weather prior to burning. Please call in on the DAY of the burn, every day you intend to burn.
  • No fires will be permitted on any public or private street, or within the right-of-way of a public or private street.

This does not include all rules and requirements for residential burning.   

For more information, reference Pahrump Town Ordinance No. 28
