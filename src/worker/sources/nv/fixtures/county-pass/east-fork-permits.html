A permit is required for all open-flame burns. Permits are issued for one year and must be renewed annually.

 

Recreational & Ceremonial Burns

Apply for your Recreational or Ceremonial Burn Permit online through Community Connect. Simply create or log in to your account, complete the permit application, and submit it electronically.

 

Agricultural & Agricultural Ditch Burns

Agricultural and Agricultural Ditch Burn Permits must be obtained in person at the East Fork Fire Protection District Office.
