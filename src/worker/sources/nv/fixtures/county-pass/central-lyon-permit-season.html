Burn Season 2026-2027

Burn season opens October 31, 2026 at sunrise. You can create your account ahead of time here on our burn permit site: 

https://www.communityconnect.io/info/nv-centrallyoncounty

Create your account and enter your address and contact information. Once your account is created you can apply for a burn permit on or after 10/31/2026.  If the site says "There is currently a burn ban in effect or your property is in a no burn zone." this is due to weather, check back the next day. You only need to apply for 1 permit for the whole season.
