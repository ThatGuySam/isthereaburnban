[Ord. No. 3-91, 5/7/1991; as amended by Ord. No. 5-91, 9/3/1991; and by Ord. No. 2024-87, 1/6/2025]
1. 
Hereafter no person shall, within those areas of Kelly Township, Union County, Pennsylvania, now or hereafter designated as Residential-Rural, Residential-Suburban, Residential-Urban, Commercial-Neighborhood, Commercial-Highway Manufacturing and Institutional on the Kelly Township Zoning Map, ignite or feed an open fire for the destruction of refuse or in the conduct of a salvage operation except as hereinafter provided:
A. 
Paper, cardboard, tree branches, leaves, yard trimmings, untreated wood and wood products, (provided the same are dry), may be burned in an open fire between sunrise and sunset on Monday, Wednesday and Friday and between sunrise and 1:00 p.m. prevailing time, on Saturday and from sunrise to sunset on the last Saturday of each month, provided that the said fire shall be contained within a non-combustible container. Said container must be properly vented and covered with a wire mesh with holes no larger than 1/2 inch wide. All such fires shall be attended at all times by a person at least 16 years of age and shall be controlled so as not to endanger real or personal property. All such fires shall be completely extinguished at the end of the herein set forth burning hours.
2. 
Open fires may be maintained upon written permission from the Kelly Township Supervisors or their authorized agent, which may include specific conditions, pursuant to written application and after notice to the Union County Emergency Management Services in the following circumstances:
A. 
By an officer of a local, state or federal governmental body or quasi-governmental body acting in his or her official capacity when the same is necessary for the prevention of a fire hazard which cannot be abated by any other means and in the preservation of the public health and safety.
B. 
By any person when, in the sole discretion of the Board of Supervisors or agent, no other practical means is available for the disposal of the material to be burned.
3. 
Notwithstanding anything herein to the contrary, no open fire shall be permitted where the same shall be a threat to safety and/or health of any person or property including the person or persons igniting, feeding or attending said open fire on their property.
4. 
Open fires may be maintained for recreational purposes and should be limited to split wood commonly burned in fireplaces, fire pits or similar facilities. Any such fire shall be contained within a metal or masonry barrel, pit or similar device which shall be no more than three feet square or three feet in diameter and shall be located not less than 25 feet from any structure nor less than 10 feet from an adjoining property line. The said fire must be supervised at all times by an adult.
5. 
Public, governmental, charitable and private organizations, and groups or similar entities, may conduct ceremonies, rallies, demonstrations, or similar events utilizing open burning such as bonfires provided the open burning is approved by and supervised by the Kelly Township Fire Chief or his/her delegated representative.
