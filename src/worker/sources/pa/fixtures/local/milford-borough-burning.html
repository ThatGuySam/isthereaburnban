No person or persons shall set or maintain any fire upon the paved portion of any of the streets, alleys or public grounds in the Borough of Milford, Pike County, Pennsylvania, or burn or cause to be burned thereon any material or substance of any kind.
A. 
Out-of-doors fires shall be permitted upon private property in the Borough, provided that the following regulations and restrictions are strictly adhered to:
(1) 
Every such out-of-doors fire shall be built in and confined to a noncombustible container covered with a screen not to exceed a two-inch mesh or built in and confined to another suitable noncombustible container.
(2) 
No such fire shall be an unsafe distance from any building or from any property line.
B. 
Notwithstanding the regulations and restrictions contained in this section, upon prior application to the Zoning Committee of the Borough Council, exceptions thereto temporary in nature may be granted in said Committee's discretion. The Zoning Committee shall consult with the Fire Chief of the Milford Fire Department before issuing any permits.
C. 
The provisions of this section shall not apply to the use of barbecue grills, fireplaces, etc., for the purposes of food preparation.
D. 
The burning of leaves and/or garbage is expressly prohibited within the Borough.
