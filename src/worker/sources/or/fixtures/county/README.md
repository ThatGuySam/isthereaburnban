- Umatilla's panel published a combined November 25–26 no-burn decision at 5:34 a.m. Pacific on November 25. Crook's Elementor page keeps all three cards in the HTML. The hidden widget class removes the two inactive cards, leaving the November 25 regulated burn day.
- Union's [public HTML Smoke Line](https://unioncountyor.gov/smoke/burnlog.php) identifies the daily decision and links to the CSV. Its companion HTML history confirms that the two bit fields represent morning and afternoon. November 24 has two zeroes and November 25 allows afternoon only. The overnight November 26 capture was still awaiting the 5:30 a.m. decision.
- Before You Burn shows Crooked River Ranch's zone as open and controlled day to day by the district. The district's [instructions](https://www.crrfire-or.gov/outdoor-burning) require registration, and its zone lists Jefferson and Deschutes. Bend Fire’s permanent city card agrees with the yard-debris prohibition under [Bend Code 5.30.005](https://bend.municipal.codes/BC/5.30.005), which separately lists exceptions. [Depoe Bay Fire](https://www.depoebayfire.com/debris-burning-open-october-15-2026) links to its zone as the source for current backyard debris restrictions.
- The DEQ layer contains 53 features. The county-specific portions of OAR 340-264-0110 and the [control-area definition](https://secure.sos.state.or.us/oard/viewSingleRule.action?ruleVrsnRsn=256251) identify the Willamette rules. The DEQ layer prints `304-264-0110` on the five seasonal rows. The [Lakeview town page](https://townoflakeview.org/fire-department/) confirms the November 1–March 1 winter closure in the urban growth boundary. [Ashland's published closure](https://www.ashlandoregon.gov/325/The-Outdoor-Burning-Window-is-Currently-) confirms the Medford–Ashland area reopens March 1.
- Klamath's signed ordinance specifies declared burn windows or a variance within the Air Quality Zone. Wallowa's Ordinance 347-17 section 6.4 limits city burn barrels to 10 a.m.–6 p.m. Section 6.5 provides a separate locally declared fire-season closure. John Day Fire's linked city guide says residents need a permit for covered outdoor burning.
