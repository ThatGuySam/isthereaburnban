FIRE PROTECTION EQUIPMENT AND CONDITIONS REQUIRED:

  • Determine that wind speed and inversion conditions are conducive to burning.
  • Monitor your fire at all times. Do not leave it unattended for any length of time.
  • DO NOT burn any material that will cause an offensive odor: no grass clippings, leaves, dead animals, garbage, plastics or other odor-causing items.
  • Only burn between 8 am and 5 pm.
  • Keep water, rake and shovel nearby while burning.
  • If the fire starts to get out of control, call 911 immediately.
