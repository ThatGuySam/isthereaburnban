Outdoor Burning for Tillamook Bay Fire & Rescue RFPD will open effective October 1st, 2026, with a Valid Burn Permit. For Tillamook Bay Fire & Rescue RFPD Patrons,  Burn Permits may be requested on The Fire Prevention page under Burn Permits. The Burn Season will end on June 30, 2027 in Tillamook Bay Fire & Rescue District.
