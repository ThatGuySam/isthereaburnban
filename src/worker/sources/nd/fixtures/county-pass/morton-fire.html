Safe Open Burning & Controlled Burn Practices

Current Morton County Burn Status

Updated November 12, 2026

The Morton County Fire Emergency and countywide Burn Ban have been rescinded. The Burn Restriction adopted January 27, 2026, is again in effect through December 2026.

This means the full countywide burn ban is no longer in effect; however, open burning is prohibited whenever:

  • The North Dakota Rangeland Fire Danger Rating is High, Very High or Extreme, or

  • A Red Flag Warning has been issued for Morton County.

Check the North Dakota burn restrictions and fire danger map before beginning any outdoor burning.

Activities prohibited during restricted conditions

When the fire danger rating is High, Very High or Extreme, or when a Red Flag Warning is in effect, the following activities are prohibited:

  • Campfires

  • Controlled burns, including wood piles, tree piles and tumbleweeds

  • Cropland and agricultural burning

  • Outdoor fireplaces and chimineas

  • Aerial and ground fireworks

  • Garbage and pit burning

  • Charcoal grills and smokers

  • Wood-fired grills and smokers

Devices allowed during restricted conditions

The following gas-fueled devices may be used only when all required safeguards are followed:

  • Gas grills

  • Gas patio fireplaces and chimineas

  • Gas camp stoves

The device must be placed on a hard, non-organic surface at least 15 feet from vegetation. A fire extinguisher or an immediately available water source must be present.

Charcoal, wood-fired and pellet-burning devices are not included in this exception.

When controlled burning is otherwise permitted

When the fire danger rating is Low or Moderate and no Red Flag Warning is in effect, residents planning a controlled or open burn should:

  1. Check with the local fire chief for the area where the burn will occur.

  2. Contact the State Emergency Communications Center at 701-328-9921 before beginning the burn. Be prepared to provide your name, telephone number, burn location and expected duration.

  3. Continuously monitor the fire. Never leave it unattended.

  4. Keep water, an extinguisher, equipment or other appropriate suppression resources immediately available.

  5. Call 911 immediately if the fire escapes or becomes difficult to control.

  6. Contact the State Emergency Communications Center again after the burn is completely extinguished.

Calling the fire chief or the State Emergency Communications Center does not authorize burning when a Morton County restriction or Red Flag Warning prohibits it.

City requirements may differ

Mandan, Flasher, Glen Ullin and Hebron have their own fire codes or burning ordinances. Residents within those city limits should contact their local fire department for current requirements.

New Salem residents should contact the city auditor at 701-843-7828 for information about requirements within city limits.

Stay informed

Fire conditions and restrictions can change quickly. Before any outdoor burning:

  • Check the current fire danger rating and Red Flag Warnings.

  • Review current Morton County notices.

  • Follow instructions from local fire and emergency-management officials.

For additional information, contact Morton County Emergency Management or your local fire department.
