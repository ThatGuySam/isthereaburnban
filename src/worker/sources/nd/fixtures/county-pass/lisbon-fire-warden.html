13-204. FIRE IN STREETS OR ALLEYS. No person or persons shall burn any straw, hay, shavings, trash, lumber, leaves, or other combustible material in any street, alley, or vacant lot except by special permission of the Fire Warden when such burning shall be done in screened metallic receptacles approved by him and under such proper safeguards as he may direct as to time and weather; provided, that the Fire Warden may issue a general permit by publication during certain seasons of the year for the burning of leaves or other material where conducted during daylight and with an adult in constant attendance.
