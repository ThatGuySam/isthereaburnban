Fire Restrictions

Pima County wildfire restrictions often align with recommendations from the Arizona Department of Forestry and Fire Management and federal land management agencies. Pima County fire restrictions apply to County-owned property – parks, campgrounds, washes, RV parks, the Fairgrounds, leased properties such as Old Tucson, and County wildlands and ranches. Pima County preserves and protects more than 250,000 acres and also has thousands of acres of urban parks.
