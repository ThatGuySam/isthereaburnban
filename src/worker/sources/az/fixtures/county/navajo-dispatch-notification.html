The Navajo County Open Burn Permits are for the unincorporated areas of Navajo County outside of a fire district. If you have any questions please contact the Navajo County Emergency Management at (928) 524-4163 or your local fire department for verification.

Prior to burning any material, the Permittee shall call the Navajo County Sheriff Office Dispatch at (928) 524-4050 and the local Fire Department. Provide each entity with name, permit number, and physical address of burn location. You must call each time you are going to burn.
