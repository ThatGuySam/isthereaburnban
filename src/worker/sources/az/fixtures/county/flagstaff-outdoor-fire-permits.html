Please note the following:

  • All grills and portable stoves that have on/off switches are allowed at private residences during all fire restriction stages. Pellet grills qualify as a device with an on/off switch.
  • In the event that areas of the Coconino National Forest are closed, sections of the Flagstaff Urban Trails System (FUTS) that provide entrance to those areas will also be closed. 
  • Per the Flagstaff WUI Code and Local Amendments to the Fire Code (City Code Section 13-13-001-0001), the Fire Chief maintains sole jurisdiction over fire safety requirements and the authority to prohibit any outdoor fire to mitigate wildland fire exposure.
  • The Wildland Urban Interface (WUI) Code is always applicable within the City of Flagstaff: No person shall build, ignite, or maintain any outdoor fire of any kind for any purpose within the City of Flagstaff, except by the authority of a written permit from the Fire Department. EXCEPTION: Outdoor fires within inhabited premises or designated campsites where such fires are in a permanent barbecue, portable barbecue, outdoor fireplace, incinerator, grill, or any other outdoor fire appliance for such purposes and are a minimum of 15 feet from any combustible materials or non fire-resistive vegetation.

The City of Flagstaff cooperates with local, county, state, and federal fire management agencies to ensure a coordinated effort is made in fire restrictions actions across jurisdictions.
