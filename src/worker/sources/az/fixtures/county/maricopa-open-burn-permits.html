Burn activities that require a permit:

  • Firefighter training
  • Agricultural land clearance or agricultural ditchbanks and fence rows
  • Disease/pest prevention (must be declared necessary by a public official)
  • Prevention of fire hazards (must be declared necessary by a public official)
  • Destruction of tumbleweeds
  • Watershed rehabilitation or control

Burn activities that do not require a permit:

  • Recreational fires between September 1 - April 30 (not allowed on declared No Burn Days)
  • Proper disposal of flags
  • Branding of animals
  • Orchard heaters
  • Display of pyrotechnics
  • Other fires may qualify- see Rule 314 for more information

Burn activities that are always prohibited:

  • Burning lawn clippings/debris
  • Burning garbage
  • Any fire during a restricted-burn period
  • Land clearance for non-agricultural properties

Applications for Open Burn permits are completed in the Dust and Miscellaneous Portal. More information about burning activities can be found on the Burning Activities page.
