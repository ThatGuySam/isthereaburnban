Residential burn permits are required to burn piles of vegetation within CAFMA's districts. Burn permits are obtained online and are valid through the end of the calendar year. 

To obtain a burn permit:

  • Visit prescottarea.burnpermits.com.
  • Create an account or log into an existing account. 
  • Verify that it is a burn day before registering a burn.
  • Stop your active burn when the fire is completely out. 

Cooking and Warming Fires: 

  • Do not require a burn permit, but may be affected by fire restrictions. 
  • Should be 25' from any structure. 
  • Total fuel area is 3' or less in diameter and 2' or less in height. 

Outdoor Fireplaces:

  • Do not require a burn permit.
  • Should be 15' from any structure.

Bonfires: 

  • Do not require a burn permit, but may be affected by fire restrictions.
  • Should be 50' from any structure.

Tips and Tricks: 

  • Build fires a safe distance from any structures.
  • Never leave any fire unattended.
  • Have a water supply and fire extinguisher nearby.
  • Fully extinguish the fire before walking away.

Commerical burn permits are required and can be obtained through the CAFMA SmartGov Customer Portal.
